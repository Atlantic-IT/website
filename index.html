Services

Practical security work, clearly scoped and plainly explained.

Threat Modeling

A structured process for identifying the most likely attack scenarios your business faces. You receive prioritized risk areas and recommended mitigations you can actually act on.

Penetration Testing

Simulated attacks against your internet-facing systems and web applications to find vulnerabilities before real attackers do. Clear findings, clear remediation steps.

Security Awareness Training

Short, frequent modules with role-specific scenarios and simulated phishing. Designed to be educational, not punitive — your team learns without the scare tactics.

Incident Response Planning

Tailored runbooks, tabletop exercises, and escalation plans so your organization knows exactly what to do when — not if — an incident occurs.

About

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting was founded in 2017 by Benjamin Sondgeroth to bring enterprise-grade security experience to small and mid-sized businesses — the organizations that need it most but are least likely to have it in-house.

Ben's background spans offensive security, risk management, and security leadership at scale:

  • Led a 28-person Security Architecture team at Morgan Stanley
  • Built and ran a global red team at CA Technologies; penetration tested 500+ systems
  • Led tabletop exercises for 50+ senior leaders; contributed to $100M+ in revenue protection
  • Conference speaker at West Point, Gridiron, SANS, and Accelerate
  • Multiple first-place finishes in capture-the-flag competitions
