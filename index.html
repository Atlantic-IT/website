About

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting was founded in 2017 by Benjamin Sondgeroth to bring practical, senior-level technology guidance to small and mid-sized businesses — the organizations that depend on IT every day but may not have a full team in-house.

Ben's background spans day-to-day IT operations, endpoint environments, architecture, risk management, offensive and defensive security, and security leadership at scale:

  • Over a decade of hands-on information technology experience.
  • Executive-level experience leading security architecture teams.
  • Built and operated a global red team, with penetration testing across thousands of systems.
  • Led tabletop exercises for 50+ senior leaders, helping protect over $100M in business revenue.
  • Conference speaker and technology educator.
  • Multiple first-place finishes in capture-the-flag cybersecurity competitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size businesses do you work with?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting works with small and mid-sized businesses — typically organizations with 10 to 500 employees that need dependable technology help but don't have a full IT team in-house.

What services do you provide and what do they do?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting offers five core services:

  • Device setup and onboarding — prepares laptops, desktops, accounts, baseline settings, and software so users can get productive quickly.
  • Cloud, email, and collaboration — setup and support for business email, file sharing, account access, and collaboration tools.
  • Networks and infrastructure — practical help with Wi-Fi, firewalls, connectivity, vendor coordination, and infrastructure planning.
  • Cybersecurity — assessments, threat modeling, penetration testing, awareness training, and risk reduction guidance.
  • Incident response and recovery — runbooks, tabletop exercises, escalation plans, and recovery guidance for technology or security incidents.

Additional information technology and security services are available upon request — reach out to discuss your specific needs.

Where is Atlantic Information Technology Consulting located?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting is based on Long Island, New York. We work with businesses locally and remotely across the United States.

How do I get started?

Reach out via email at ben@atlanticit.org to start a conversation about your IT needs. We'll discuss your current situation, identify priorities, and scope an engagement that fits your budget and timeline.
