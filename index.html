Frequently Asked Questions

What size businesses do you work with?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting works with small and mid-sized businesses — typically organizations with 10 to 500 employees that need enterprise-grade security expertise but don't have a full security team in-house.

What services do you provide and what do they do?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting offers four core services:

  • Threat modeling — identifies your most likely attack scenarios and gives you prioritized, actionable mitigations.
  • Penetration testing — simulated attacks against your systems and web applications, with a clear findings report and remediation steps.
  • Security awareness training — short, role-specific modules and simulated phishing, educational rather than punitive.
  • Incident response planning — tailored runbooks, tabletop exercises, and escalation plans so your team knows what to do when an incident hits.

General information technology services and additional security services are available upon request — reach out to discuss your specific needs.

Where is Atlantic Information Technology Consulting located?

Atlantic Information Technology Consulting is based on Long Island, New York. We work with businesses locally and remotely across the United States.

How do I get started?

Reach out via email at ben@atlanticit.org to start a conversation about your security needs. We'll discuss your current situation, identify priorities, and scope an engagement that fits your budget and timeline.
